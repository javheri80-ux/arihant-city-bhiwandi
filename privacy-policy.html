Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Welcome to arihantcity.site (the "Website"), owned and operated by an Authorized Channel Partner / Marketing Partner for Arihant City Kalyan Bhiwandi. We are committed to safeguarding your personal data and ensuring compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), Google Ads Transparency & User Safety Policies, and applicable privacy regulations.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse this site or submit enquiry forms for pricing details, floor plans, or site visits, we may collect the following user-submitted personal information:

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Phone Number / Contact Number
  • Email Address
  • Interested Configuration (e.g., 1 BHK Luxury, 2 BHK Elite, 3 BHK Jodi)
  • Preferred Time for Free Pick-and-Drop Site Visits

2. How We Use Your Information & Consent

By providing your contact details on arihantcity.site, you grant explicit consent to our sales advisory team and developer marketing representatives to:

  • Contact you via telephone call, SMS, WhatsApp messages, or email to share verified pricing sheets, brochures, and layout drawings.
  • Coordinate complimentary site visits with vehicle pick-up and drop-off services.
  • Provide updates on construction milestones, MahaRERA filings, bank loan sanction schemes, and festive launch discounts.
  • Validate your enquiry with the official developer (Arihant Group) for official booking records and inventory reservation.

3. Google Ads, Conversion Tracking & Cookies

This website employs Google Tag Manager, Google Ads conversion tracking, and analytics cookies. These tracking technologies help measure campaign performance and deliver relevant, non-intrusive advertisements. These cookies record aggregated, non-personally identifiable data (browser type, device category, geographic region, time spent on pages). You have the option to accept or decline cookies via your browser settings at any time without restricting your basic access to the website.

4. Data Confidentiality & Zero Third-Party Sale Policy

We value your trust. We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information to third-party telemarketers or external broker databases. Your data is used exclusively for property consulting regarding Arihant City Kalyan Bhiwandi. Secure SSL (HTTPS) encryption protocols protect your information during transmission.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You have the full right to opt out of promotional communications, request review of your collected contact information, or request permanent deletion of your data from our active CRM systems. To exercise these rights, please email us at javheri80@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Removal Request".

6. Policy Updates & Modifications
